THE GOLDEN RULE

Start with the problem.
Not the model.

Look for repeated friction: people comparing things manually, waiting on hold, copying data between apps or repeating the same information. Pick one user and one measurable outcome.

Building a voice agent?

Compare a cascaded pipeline (speech-to-text → model → text-to-speech) with real-time speech-to-speech. Test Tamil and English, noisy environments, latency, interruptions and fallback to a human. A free framework does not mean free phone calls or hosting.
